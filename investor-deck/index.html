06 Trust layer

Built for the person
who has to say yes.

The parent, the teacher, the principal. Trust is the product moat — not a compliance page.

◉

Real identity

OCR reads the document. Name and address must match the account, with a document-number pattern and confidence threshold.

  • Image deleted after verification
  • Hash, confidence and checks stored
  • Reviewed reasons, never raw pixels
Document verification
◇

Guardian consent

A secure, shareable link lets a guardian approve or decline. The student cannot self-approve, and a second decision is rejected.

  • Asynchronous approval
  • Every decision logged
  • Notifications on change
Consent infrastructure
▣

Institutional control

Admin-granted access by email. Classroom data is scoped to its owner. Publishing is a deliberate admin action.

  • Role-checked APIs
  • IDOR-guarded classroom views
  • Admin-only content publishing
Access & governance
No real money No deposits. No withdrawals. No brokerage integration. Credits buy simulated capital inside a closed loop.
